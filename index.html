THE SOCIETY OF WOMEN ENGINEERS (SWE)

was founded in 1950 in an effort to empower women scientists and engineers.
This organization values professional excellence, integrity, and mutual support.
Since this organization's founding, there are now around 50,000 members worldwide.

Some members promoting SWE at our Activities Fair!

CLARKSON'S CHAPTER OF SWE

was founded in 1973, making it the first chapter of SWE in New York state.
Our chapter aims to strengthen inclusivity while providing academic, professional,
and social support for women in engineering at Clarkson.
